Linkshelf needs its database before anyone can sign up. This takes about five minutes, once.

  1. Create a free project at supabase.com.
  2. Open SQL Editor, paste everything from schema.sql, and click Run.
  3. Click Connect in your project and copy the project URL and the publishable key.
  4. Paste them at the top of shared.js and upload it again.
